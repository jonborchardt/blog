Hackathon Aug 2014 Adap.TV

Project: Dynamic spend map
Front End: Jon Borchardt
Data: Sawin Lee, Dyng Au, Brendan Kitts

Objective

Show a map of advertising spending over time between two companies.

Output

Using D3.js we are rendering a map of the united states and showing a circle at each market were we detect spend for Coke or Pepsi.
Blue is Pepsi, red is Coke. The size of the circle represents how much money is being spent in that area.
The solid circles represent local spend in a specific market. The transparent circles represent spend in a state for a national airing.
The slider at the top specifies what time to display.

Coke vs Pepsi TV ad spend for 24 hours (4/28/2014)
