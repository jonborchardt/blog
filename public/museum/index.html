The Museum

Preserved demos from 2001-2018, served as they were.

  • Rotation Buys

    A 2013 crossfilter dashboard: drag the timeline strip and every chart refilters live.

  • Industry Tracker

    A 2013 line-with-focus chart, replacing a Flash-based industry tracker with D3.

  • Programs

    Another 2013 crossfilter dashboard: drag the timeline strip and every program chart refilters live.

  • Media Landscape Explorer

    A 2013 dc.js dashboard: filter the media landscape by type, daypart, tRatio and impressions.

  • Web Spike Explorer

    A 2013 nvd3 chart: drag the scale handle to rescale the impressions spike.

  • MapHack (Soda War)

    A 2014 hackathon demo: a D3 choropleth animating Coke vs. Pepsi TV ad spend across a day.

  • PD Industry Ratings

    A 2013 crossfilter dashboard: drag the Time Period strip and agency rankings, bubbles and histograms all refilter live.

  • PD Sell Side

    A 2013 crossfilter dashboard behind a granted sell-side ad-pricing patent: filter by station, day, hour and industry, and the 15,502-row program grid refilters live.

  • Adsplore

    A screenshot gallery: a wizard flow scoring every TV ad run by selected brands against targeted populations, with crossfilter breakdowns by daypart, network and market.

  • Population Reporting

    A screenshot gallery: consumer-demographics charts comparing Total People vs. Total Buyers by age, education, income and home market value.

  • Fatal Encounters

    A 2014 AngularJS crossfilter dashboard: drag the Filter by Month brush and the map and every chart refilter live. The data is real, from fatalencounters.org.
